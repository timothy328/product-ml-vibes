# Product ML Vibes

A practical product design and delivery workspace for product managers (PMs),
product designers (PDs), and product owners (POs). It is designed for teams
that need to connect customer problems, product decisions, usable experiences,
technical delivery, and measurable outcomes.

## How to use this repository

Start with the customer problem and desired outcome, then move through
discovery, definition, design, validation, delivery, and measurement. Not
every initiative needs every artifact; choose the lightest process that creates
shared understanding and reduces meaningful risk.

Each concept folder contains a README describing:

- When to use the practice
- What a strong artifact should include
- Who typically owns or contributes to it
- How it connects to the next product decision

## Working principles

1. Start with a meaningful customer problem, not a predetermined feature.
2. Make assumptions, decisions, tradeoffs, and open questions visible.
3. Use research and data to inform judgment; do not outsource judgment to a
   single metric or framework.
4. Design with engineering and operations early enough to discover constraints.
5. Validate the riskiest assumptions before investing in polish or scale.
6. Define success before launch and inspect outcomes after launch.
7. Make artifacts concise, findable, inclusive, and useful to the team that
   must act on them.

## Role guide

- **PM:** owns the problem, product strategy, prioritization, outcomes, and
  cross-functional alignment.
- **PD:** owns or co-owns user understanding, interaction and visual design,
  prototypes, and experience quality.
- **PO:** turns prioritized outcomes into an actionable backlog, clarifies
  scope, and supports delivery decisions with the team.

Ownership varies by organization. The important rule is to assign one directly
responsible owner for each decision and invite the right contributors.
