# Channel Strategies

Choose channels based on audience behavior, message fit, economics, reach,
control, learning value, and operational capacity. Each channel plan should
state:

- Target audience and customer journey stage
- Objective and value proposition
- Content or offer format
- Testable hypothesis and experiment design
- Budget, cadence, owner, and dependencies
- Primary metric, guardrails, attribution approach, and stop criteria

Do not assume that activity, impressions, followers, or clicks represent
incremental customer value.
